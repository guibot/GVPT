    Media

    Drag videos and PNGs here

    Videos are converted automatically (H.264, no audio). A layer can only use them once the conversion finishes. PNGs are used as masks.

    Video conversion

    When off, videos are kept as uploaded (H.264, VP8, VP9 or AV1 in .mp4, .m4v, .mov or .webm), for example already compressed with HandBrake. Other formats are still converted.

    The video is scaled down to fit inside this box, keeping its proportions (never enlarged). If a video is portrait and the box is landscape (or the other way round), the box is turned to match.

    Applies to the next videos you upload. Pick a smaller size for small videos: it saves a lot of CPU.
